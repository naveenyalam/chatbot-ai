
=== Testing English ===
Prompt: What is artificial intelligence?
Response: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines that can perform tasks that traditionally require human intelligence. This includes the ability to perceive the world around them, understand and learn from the information they receive, and make decisions or solve problems based on that information. Some of the key subfields of AI include machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics.

AI can be classified into two main categories: narrow (or weak) AI and general (or strong) AI.

1. Narrow AI, also known as weak AI, is designed to perform a specific task. It can be used in applications such as virtual personal assistants (like Siri or Alexa), recommendation systems, or image recognition software.

2. General AI, also known as strong AI, is capable of thinking like a human being. It has the potential to understand language, solve complex problems, and make decisions on its own without being explicitly programmed.

While current AI systems are highly advanced and can perform tasks that were once thought to be exclusive to humans, achieving general AI remains a significant challenge for researchers and technology developers.
